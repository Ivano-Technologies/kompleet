About KOMPLEET

KOMPLEET is a simple bookkeeping and invoicing web app built for Nigerian individuals and growing businesses. Import and categorize transactions, track spending, send invoices, and export clean financial reports from one place.

🧾 Easy Invoicing

Create professional invoices, send them to customers, and record payment when they settle.

📊 Professional Reports

Generate P&L statements, balance sheets, and other clean financial reports ready to share with your accountant.

📈 Transaction Management

Import and categorize transactions, track income and expenses, and maintain accurate records.

💸 Expense Tracking

Sort every expense into clear categories so you always know where your money goes.

Why We Use Google Sign-In

🔐 Secure & Convenient Authentication

KOMPLEET uses Google Sign-In to provide you with a secure, fast, and convenient way to access your account. When you sign in with Google, we only request access to your:

  • Email address: to create and identify your account
  • Name: to personalize your experience
  • Profile picture (optional): to display on your account

We do NOT access: Your Google Drive, Gmail, Calendar, or any other Google services. We only use Google for authentication purposes.
