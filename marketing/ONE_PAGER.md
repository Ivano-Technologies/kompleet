## What is KOMPLEET?

KOMPLEET is Nigeria's premier tax compliance and financial management platform, designed specifically for businesses and individuals navigating the 2026 Nigerian Tax Act. The platform combines powerful transaction management, automated tax calculations, NRS-compliant e-invoicing, and comprehensive reporting into one seamless solution.

Whether you're a small business owner, freelancer, or corporate finance professional, KOMPLEET provides everything you need to stay compliant, organized, and audit-ready.

## Core Features

**Transaction Management** - Track income and expenses across 18 categories with real-time insights. Import transactions via CSV, categorize automatically, and maintain comprehensive financial records that sync across all devices.

**Tax Calculators** - Six professional calculators covering Business Tax (CIT with tiered rates), Individual Tax (PIT with progressive brackets), VAT (7.5% with include/exclude toggle), Capital Allowances (straight-line and reducing balance depreciation), Stamp Duty (5 document types), and Property Tax (5 property types). All calculators follow 2026 Nigerian Tax Act rates with real-time calculations and formatted results.

**E-Invoicing System** - Create NRS-compliant invoices in seconds with automatic invoice numbering (INV-2026-0001 format), VAT calculation at 7.5%, digital signatures using RSA-2048 encryption, QR codes for verification, professional PDF generation, and tamper-evident storage. The system ensures invoices are immutable after issuance and automatically archives them for 7 years as required by law.

**Reports & Analytics** - Generate Tax Summary reports with effective tax rate calculations, Balance Sheet reports showing assets, liabilities, and net worth, and Profit & Loss statements with revenue breakdown and top expense categories. All reports support PDF export with professional formatting and KOMPLEET branding.

**Smart Reminders** - Never miss a tax deadline with automated notifications at 30, 14, 7, and 1 day before major deadlines. The system includes the complete 2026 Nigerian tax calendar with urgency indicators for deadlines within 7 days.

**Multi-Year Data Management** - Track financial performance across multiple years with a global year selector, year-over-year comparison dashboard, and comprehensive export system supporting CSV, Excel, Word, and ZIP formats. All exports are NDPR-compliant with proper data protection.

## Technical Excellence

**Security First** - Bank-level encryption protects all financial data. Row-Level Security (RLS) policies ensure complete data isolation between users. Digital signatures use RSA-2048 with AES-256-GCM encrypted key storage. All invoice operations are logged in comprehensive audit trails.

**Performance Optimized** - Invoice generation completes in under 2 seconds. Year switching happens in under 300 milliseconds. The platform handles thousands of transactions without slowdown.

**NDPR Compliant** - All data handling follows Nigeria Data Protection Regulation requirements. Users maintain full control over their data with transparent privacy policies and secure data export options.

**2026 Tax Act Aligned** - Every calculator, form, and deadline reflects the latest Nigerian tax regulations. The platform updates automatically as regulations evolve.
