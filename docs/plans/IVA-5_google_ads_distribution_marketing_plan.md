# IVA-5: KOMPLEET Distribution and Marketing Plan (Google Ads + Full Funnel)

## 1) Goal and Success Criteria

Primary goal: launch a first acquisition engine for KOMPLEET with Google Ads as the high-intent channel, then compound growth via partnerships, content, and lifecycle marketing.

### 90-day outcome targets

- Build a repeatable paid search funnel with stable cost per qualified lead (CPQL)
- Generate first consistent pipeline of demo requests and/or trial signups
- Create channel mix so paid traffic is not the only source of growth
- Establish baseline conversion tracking for campaign optimization

## 2) Launch Readiness Gate (Decide Before Spending)

Use one of these objectives based on product readiness:

1. **If product onboarding is ready now**  
   Use objective: **Trial signup / account creation**
2. **If onboarding is not fully ready**  
   Use objective: **Demo booking / waitlist signups**

Do not run conversion-optimized campaigns until the final conversion action is fully working end-to-end.

## 3) What to Enter in Google Ads "About your business"

Suggested setup for the signup flow:

- Business name: `KOMPLEET`
- Website: `https://kompleet.ng` (or current production URL)
- Business category: `Accounting software` (or closest available equivalent)
- Business goal (recommended): `Get leads` first, then expand to `Website traffic` and `Sales`

## 4) Campaign Architecture (First 6 Weeks)

Start simple and high-intent. Build depth only after baseline conversion data is clean.

### Campaign 1: Search - Tax Compliance Intent (Core)

- Network: Google Search only (disable Display expansion initially)
- Geo: Nigeria (prioritize Lagos, Abuja, Port Harcourt, Ibadan, Kano)
- Language: English
- Bid strategy:
  - Week 1-2: Maximize Clicks with CPC guardrail
  - Week 3+: Maximize Conversions (after conversion data quality is validated)
- Ad schedule: 6:00-22:00 local time (adjust using performance by hour)

Ad groups:

1. Tax compliance software
2. VAT and tax calculator
3. SME tax filing
4. E-invoicing and invoice compliance

### Campaign 2: Brand + Navigational Protection

- Capture branded searches and prevent competitor interception.
- Tight exact/phrase match only.
- Lower budget than Campaign 1, but always-on.

### Campaign 3: Remarketing (after Week 3)

- Audience: site visitors without conversion in last 30 days
- Channels: Search remarketing first; add YouTube/Display only if creative is ready
- Message focus: trust, compliance urgency, demo/trial CTA

## 6) Ad Copy Framework (Responsive Search Ads)

Use messaging pillars already aligned with brand materials:

1. Compliance confidence
2. Time savings
3. Nigerian context relevance
4. Clear CTA

### Headline ideas

- Nigerian Tax Compliance, Simplified
- Automate VAT and Tax Calculations
- Stay Ready for 2026 Tax Rules
- E-Invoicing for Nigerian Businesses
- Track Records, Filings, Compliance
- Tax Filing Support for SMEs
- All-in-One Tax and Finance Workflow
- Start Your KOMPLEET Trial

### Description ideas

- Keep records, filings, and compliance in one workflow built for Nigerian businesses.
- Reduce manual tax work with guided calculations and deadline reminders.
- Start with a trial or book a walkthrough to see how KOMPLEET fits your workflow.
- Built for SMEs, founders, and finance teams that need compliance without complexity.

## 7) Landing Page Requirements (Critical)

Each ad group should map to one focused landing experience.

Required above-the-fold elements:

- Strong headline matching keyword intent
- Single primary CTA (trial signup or book demo)
- Trust signals: NRS/NDPR/CAC references where appropriate
- Product proof: feature bullets + screenshots
- Compliance disclaimer and support contact

Conversion hygiene:

- Keep form to 3-5 fields max
- Add WhatsApp or call CTA for high-intent users
- Include FAQ section to reduce objections
- Ensure mobile page speed and no broken form flows

## 8) Budget and Pacing

Choose one launch band based on cash constraints:

- Lean test: `NGN 450,000/month` (~NGN 15,000/day)
- Standard test: `NGN 1,050,000/month` (~NGN 35,000/day)
- Growth test: `NGN 2,250,000/month` (~NGN 75,000/day)

Suggested split (first month):

- 70% Search core intent
- 15% Brand protection
- 15% Remarketing (start once audience size is viable)

Reallocate weekly based on CPQL and conversion rate by campaign/ad group.

## 9) Full Distribution Plan Beyond Google Ads

Paid ads should be one pillar, not the whole strategy.

### A) Partnerships (highest leverage for trust)

- Tax advisors and accounting firms (referral partner program)
- SME communities and founder groups
- Business registration and compliance service providers
- Co-marketing webinars: "How to stay compliant in 2026"

### B) Content and SEO

- Build intent pages around:
  - VAT calculator Nigeria
  - CIT calculator Nigeria
  - PIT calculator Nigeria
  - Tax filing checklist for SMEs
- Publish practical compliance guides with downloadable checklists
- Add internal links from guides to conversion pages

### C) Outbound and Sales-assisted

- Founder/finance lead outreach to SMEs in priority sectors
- Offer a 20-minute compliance walkthrough
- Follow a 3-touch sequence (email + WhatsApp + call)

### D) Lifecycle and Retention

- Welcome email sequence (Day 0, 2, 7)
- Activation nudges tied to key actions (first transaction, first report, first calculator run)
- Deadline reminder campaigns to drive repeat usage

## 10) Tracking and Measurement (Non-Negotiable)

Implement and validate before scaling spend:

Primary conversion events:

1. `trial_signup_completed` OR `demo_booked`
2. `qualified_lead_submitted`
3. `contact_whatsapp_click` (secondary)

Recommended measurement stack:

- Google Ads conversion tracking
- GA4 with UTM discipline
- Weekly reporting sheet with:
  - spend
  - clicks
  - CTR
  - CPC
  - landing page conversion rate
  - CPQL
  - qualified lead volume

Target operating ranges for first 6 weeks:

- CTR: > 4% on high-intent search groups
- Landing page CVR: > 5% for warm intent, > 2.5% for colder intent
- Search term relevance: rising exact-match conversion share over time

## 11) 30-60-90 Day Execution Plan

### Days 1-14 (Foundation)

- Launch Campaign 1 + Campaign 2
- Validate event tracking and attribution
- Ship 1 dedicated landing page per core ad group
- Start search term mining and negative keyword expansion

### Days 15-45 (Optimization)

- Add remarketing campaign
- Pause weak keywords and shift budget to converting clusters
- A/B test ad copy (compliance-led vs time-saving-led)
- Launch 2-3 SEO pages for high-intent calculator queries

### Days 46-90 (Scale)

- Expand winning keyword clusters
- Add partner webinar cadence (bi-weekly)
- Introduce simple referral incentive for accountants/consultants
- Build lead scoring and handoff for sales follow-up speed

## 12) Weekly Operating Cadence

- Monday: performance review + budget reallocation
- Wednesday: search term cleanup + negative updates
- Friday: landing page and ad copy experiment review
- Monthly: channel mix review (paid vs partnerships vs organic)

## 13) Risks and Mitigations

1. **Low conversion despite clicks**  
   Mitigation: tighten keyword intent, improve landing page message match, simplify form
2. **High CPC in broad queries**  
   Mitigation: shift to phrase/exact, expand negatives, increase long-tail terms
3. **Lead quality issues**  
   Mitigation: qualify with form fields, better ad copy pre-qualification, refine geo/device bid modifiers
4. **Over-reliance on paid traffic**  
   Mitigation: enforce parallel partnership + content engine from Week 1
